🟦 Arrays
In C programming , Array is a linear collection of homogeneous elements(yaani same data type rakhne waale elements)  called as Arrays. Arrays is also known as "subscript variable" .

🟨 History of Arrays ( Yaani yeh arrays ka concept aaya kaha se ? )
Toh let say hume bola jaata hai ki . "Write a program to calculate average of three numbers"
Toh hum a , b , c three variables declare karenge and unme scanf() se input lenge and uspar ek operation karenge        a+b+c/3 and we get the average.

But let say hume bola jaat hai : "Write a program to calculate average of 100 numbers"
Toh ab hum kya karenge , kya hum ab ek-ek karke hundred variables declare karenge a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h...... and then 100 lines of scanf() yaani 100 times scanf() likhenge just to take input in these 100 variables one by one . Yeh toh phir bahut problematic ho jaayega , pehli problem aayegi "code repetition" ho jaayega scanf() ka and "program bhi bina matlab ke bada ban jaayega" , then concept aaya "Arrays" ka jo yeh problem solve karte the .
