Toh kyuki yeh data int hai toh hum bolenege ki "we use int" data type ka use karenge and teen variables banayenge int a , b , c; and "a" me 28 , "b" me 08 and "c" me 2026 store kar denge .But problem yaha yeh hai ki "Real life me toh 28/08/2026 is pure ko ek single data ki tarah treat kiya jaa raha hai" . Kyuki ho sakta hai hai ki 10 dates ko store karna ho , tab toh 30 variables banane padenge and wo bhi unorganized as each subpart of a date is present in a isolated single variables . Toh Humne phir socha ki chalo , dikkat yahi hai naa ki jyada variables ho jaa rahe hai ek single data(which is "date") ko store karne ek liye . But yeh bhi galat approach hai because of two reasons : Pehli baat toh yeh hai ki 28 , 08 , 2026 in teeno kaa nature alag-alag hai yaani 28 is a day , 08 is month , 2026 is a year . And as we know "array me hamesha same nature ka data daalte hai taaki traverse kar sake loop se yaani yaa toh sirf         day, day ,day      yaa toh     month,month,month      yaa      year,year,year  " . Now , ab hum kya kare , ab hum naa toh variables declare kar sakte hai itne saare , naa hi array use kar sakte hai as arrays in C language are homogeneous . Here , comes  a new concept of "Structures" or "Struct" which solves this exact problem .

🟡 What is struct ?
📀 Struct is a way to group heterogeneous data or we can say Struct is a collection of dissimilar elements .
📀 Matlab struct is same like arrays but "array is a collection of data which is of same data type and of same nature" .
   But "struct is a collection of data which is may or maynot be of same data type and may or maynot be of same nature".
   Kyuki Real life me most of data single hi hota hai , But jaruri nahi hai ki uske sub parts same data type ka hi ho and same nature ke hi ho like we have "date" which we treat as a single data but in actual it is made of subparts which are not if same nature . Toh Data single hi hai but jin elements yaa subparts se wo single data bana hai , wo jaruri nahi hai ki unka data type yaa nature of data same ho . Struct ka concept inhi type ke data ko store karne ke liye banaya gaya tha .
📀 Defining a struct means creating a custom data type .
📀 "Struct" is a concept in C language similiar to we have "objects" in javascript .
